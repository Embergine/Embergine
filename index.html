What changed in the 2025 guidelines

  • IV first; IO when IV is not quick. Endotracheal drugs removed. No central line during CPR.
  • Adrenaline as early as possible in non-shockable rhythms; after the 2nd shock in shockable rhythms.
  • Persistent VF/pVT (≥3 shocks): optimise basics, change vector to antero-posterior; double sequential defibrillation may be considered (AHA 2b).
  • Calcium, bicarbonate, steroids: not routine; calcium has an ERC strong recommendation against in OHCA.
  • POCUS only by experienced hands, never at the cost of compressions, never the sole reason to stop.
  • Physiology-guided CPR (ERC): EtCO₂ ≥25 mmHg and diastolic ≥30 mmHg with an arterial line; 50–100 µg adrenaline boluses then.
  • Cardioversion of AF/flutter starts at ≥200 J (AHA); sotalol removed from stable wide-complex options.
  • Post-ROSC: fever prevention ≤37.5 °C for 36–72 h (ERC) or 32–37.5 °C (AHA); MAP ≥65; SpO₂ 92–98 % (ERC 94–98); normocapnia; angiography now only for ST-elevation; prognosticate ≥72 h, multimodally.
